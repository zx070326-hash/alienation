CHAPTER / PREPRINT

ETALON

A public paper on drug-design software. The agent may change the research route. The execution record still keeps “not finished” apart from “the molecule did not work.” It is a preprint, not a journal article.

In one sentence

When a coding agent does drug design, finished, checked, and unfavorable are three different notes. An unfinished run is not stored as a negative result.

Why it matters, and what is hard

Drug design looks up evidence, screens molecules, and runs physical models at once. The route can change, and a computation often stops halfway. Writing that stop as “this molecule is useless” bends every later judgment.

My role

Co-first author (equal contribution) from Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University. The code is open-sourced in public repositories.

How the record splits

The public names are MolQuarry, MolCascade, and PRISM. MolQuarry keeps chemical and biological evidence. MolCascade handles preparation, screening, and docking. PRISM handles generation and physical modeling. The execution layer checks an operation before it is sent, and the output keeps completion, verification, and admission as separate notes.

What this chapter is not

It is not a peer-reviewed paper, a clinical result, or a prize. The preprint header says the data may be preliminary. This page does not reprint the abstract, and it does not list ranks from the screening inside the paper.
